Privacy Policy — Ledgr

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Ledgr is a personal finance dashboard for one user. This policy explains what the Ledgr statement sync does with your data.

What the app reads

The sync reads bank and credit card statement PDF files from one Google Drive folder that its owner chooses. It uses read-only Google Drive access and does not read, change or delete any other file.

What the app stores

Statement PDFs are opened in memory and are never saved to disk or to the repository. From each statement the app keeps only summary figures: monthly income, spending, savings, balances and transaction counts. These are stored in the owner's Cloudflare account and published on the owner's dashboard.

What the app does not do

The app does not sell, share or send your data to anyone else. It does not use your data for advertising. It does not keep transaction descriptions on the public website.

Access and deletion

You can revoke the app's Google access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. The owner can delete the stored figures from the Cloudflare KV store and the repository.
